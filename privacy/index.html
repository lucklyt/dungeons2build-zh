01 What we collect

Dungeons2Build doesn't ask you for anything that identifies you. There are no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no comment sections, and no newsletter. You can read the whole site without handing over a name, an email address, or any other personal detail.

02 Hosting & server logs

This site is static and hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like virtually every website, Cloudflare automatically processes standard request data — your IP address, user agent, timestamps, and the URL you requested — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We don't combine this data with anything else, and we don't use it to identify individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) for details on how they handle it.

03 Cookies & local storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's local or session storage. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising trackers, and no third-party embeds on any page.

If that ever changes — for example, if we add analytics or another tool that stores data — this page will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date below the title.

04 Links to other sites

Dungeons2Build links out to official pages for the game: the official Minecraft Dungeons II page (opens in a new tab) and the Minecraft store (opens in a new tab).

Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we have no control over. We'd encourage reading them if you're unsure how a site you land on handles your data.

05 Children's privacy

Dungeons2Build is a general-audience site. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone who visits, including children under 13 — largely because we don't collect personal data from anyone at all (see What we collect).

06 Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA may give you rights over your personal data, including the right to access it, delete it, or object to how it's used.

Because Dungeons2Build holds no personal data about visitors, there is normally nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. The only request-level data involved is the standard traffic data Cloudflare processes to deliver the site (see Hosting & server logs), which is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date at the top of the page. We won't make changes retroactively or without updating that date.

As the site grows it may add advertising or optional accounts. If it does, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what changes before those features go live.
