关于 DUNGEONS2BUILD

An independent, fan-made build companion for Minecraft Dungeons II.

What This Site Is

Dungeons2Build covers Minecraft Dungeons II with facts we can source: official announcements first, then documented launch data and what players find in the released game. Anything that isn't backed up yet is labelled "Not confirmed yet" instead of passed off as fact.

Since launch on September 29, 2026, the site runs four tools: a gear database, abuild lab, a tier list and anedition picker, plus ready-made builds.

How We Check Facts

  • Official sources first

    We verify against minecraft.net, the official store, Minecraft Live, and the Xbox Games Showcase.

  • Cross-checked

    Every detail is checked against more than one source before it goes on the page.

  • Labeled honestly

    Anything not yet verified is marked unverified — never presented as confirmed.

What We Won't Do

  • No invented stats.
  • No leaks — only officially announced material.
  • No official logos or box art.
  • All artwork on this site is original.
