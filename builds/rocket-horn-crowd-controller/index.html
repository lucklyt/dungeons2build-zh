Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller

AGrade A tier · GreatCrowd control

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

A-tier control pick

Rocket and Horn is the crowd-control build for Minecraft Dungeons 2: the Humbling Horn knocks a pack down, then Firework Arrow rockets and a frost pouch finish it while it is stuck. It works at range, which is why players prefer it against tanky mobs.

How do you play the Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller?

Control from a distance. You decide where the fight happens: shove the pack into a clump, freeze or knock it down, then drop explosives on the pile while your armor shortens the cooldowns.

  1. Open with the frost pouchThrow Pouch of Frost at the edge of the pack. Gravity Pulse drags the group in and the blizzard slows them.
  2. Horn when they close inAs enemies reach you, blow the Humbling Horn. Everything nearby goes down, which buys a few seconds.
  3. Rocket the pileFire the Firework Arrow into the knocked-down clump. With piercing and the fedora's extra projectiles, one press hits many.
  4. Back off with SpringloadEach artifact use gives a speed boost. Use it to open distance before the next cooldown is ready.
  5. Fill gaps with arrowsArrows recharge by themselves, so shoot between artifacts. The Shackler's chain can pin something that survives.

Where does the Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller shine and struggle?

Strengths

  • Handles big packs before they reach you
  • Both main artifacts work at range
  • Armor shortens cooldowns, so the loop repeats often
  • Works as a solo build and does not need teammates to function

Weaknesses

  • Every step spends souls, so a long fight can run dry
  • Weak if you get caught in melee with no cooldown ready
  • Single big targets take less advantage of the crowd tools

What is the Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller's job in co-op?

Control caster. You keep the pack off the melee players and hand them a stunned, clustered group. Do not throw explosives at close teammates, since one friendly-fire item exists in the game and it is a different one.

Quick answers about the Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller

Why do players like Rocket and Horn so much?

Both work from range and they cover each other. The horn takes enemies out of the fight for a moment, and the rocket punishes the crowd while it is down.

When do I get the second artifact slot?

After the lost-merchant quest in Howling Woods. Until then, equip the rocket and add the horn once the second slot opens.

Does Gravity Pulse fight against the Horn?

It pulls, the horn pushes, so order matters. Let the pull land first, then blow the horn to drop the clumped group.

Not confirmed yet for the Rocket and Horn Crowd Controller

  • We could not confirm which ranged weapon families accept Firework Arrow. Footage shows a bow; The Shackler's class is unchecked.
