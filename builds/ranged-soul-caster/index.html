Ranged Soul Caster Mage Build

BGrade B tier · SolidRanged

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

B-tier ranged pick

The ranged soul caster is the mage-style build for Minecraft Dungeons 2. You gather extra souls with a Soul Reaper, then spend them on the Soul Harvester, Cinder Scepter and Winter Staff, and armor makes each artifact hit harder.

What does a fight look like with the Ranged Soul Caster Mage Build?

Charge, then release. You farm souls with melee hits, then spend them in big bursts. Armor and talismans raise soul capacity and artifact power.

  1. Farm the barMelee weak enemies with Soul Reaper first. The 60% bonus, the talisman and the soul chip all fill the bar quicker.
  2. Charge the ScepterHold the Cinder Scepter to charge it, then release into the biggest group. Charging is the vulnerable moment, so do it from cover.
  3. Hex the tough oneCharge Soul Harvester and release on the elite you want weakened. Follow with ranged shots while the hex is on.
  4. Blizzard to buy timeWinter Staff slows and damages a group. Use it when something reaches you, then back off with the Springload speed boost.
  5. Potion for soulsAncient Alchemy lets potions grant souls even at full health. Drink between casts to refill the bar.

Burst damage dealer. Wait for a teammate to hold attention, then charge and release. The blizzard helps a group that is being swarmed.

Common questions on the Ranged Soul Caster Mage Build

What are souls used for?

Souls are a resource collected in combat that fuels artifacts. They drop as you kill, and Soul Reaper, Twisted Tooth and the Soul Chip all help you gather and hold more.

Is the Soul Caster a good solo build?

It can be, since the Winter Staff and Cinder Scepter cover single and group targets. It relies on managing the soul bar, so a rough start is normal.

Is Sculker's Bane better than Soul Reaper here?

Sculker's Bane adds 20% damage to soul attacks. That is stronger if your artifacts count as soul attacks, which we have not confirmed, so we default to the fuel weapon.

What is still unconfirmed on the Ranged Soul Caster Mage Build?

  • Whether Sculker's Bane boosts artifact soul attacks or only weapon soul attacks is not confirmed.
  • Soul Corruptor Mask has no listed effect, so its value in this build is a guess.
