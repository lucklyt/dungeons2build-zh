Pack Leader Summoner Build

CGrade C tier · UnprovenSummoner

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

C-tier summoner pick

The pack leader companion build for Minecraft Dungeons 2 fills your talisman slots with a wolf, an iron golem and a Prickle, then uses Fly Agaric and Woodsprite armor to make them hit harder and die less. It is fun, and it is unproven.

How do you play the Pack Leader Summoner Build?

Let your team of pets do the work. You stay close, keep them alive and add a little poison and a couple of tools, and the animals carry the damage.

  1. Summon firstZone in with all three talismans active. Companions are passive summons, so the pack is ready before the first pull.
  2. Knock down, then step backBlow the horn as the pack closes and let your animals bite the group while it is down.
  3. Coat the targetUse Blight Bangle on the enemy your pets are already chasing so the poison stacks with their hits.
  4. Shield when it gets loudPlace Totem of Shielding between the pack and any ranged attackers. It protects you and the animals.
  5. Rest between pullsStep out of combat for five seconds. The Fly Agaric coat heals you fast while the pets recover.

Where does the Pack Leader Summoner Build shine and struggle?

Strengths

  • Three summons take attention off you
  • Two armor sets are aimed squarely at companions
  • Low mechanical demand, so it suits newer players
  • Healing out of combat is very fast

Weaknesses

  • Companion damage numbers are unpublished
  • Bosses can wipe pets quickly
  • Little burst, so long fights against single targets drag

What is the Pack Leader Summoner Build's job in co-op?

Flexible hybrid. The pets add damage in the background so you can spend your attention on reviving or positioning. Not a replacement for a dedicated healer or tank.

Quick answers about the Pack Leader Summoner Build

How do I get the wolf, golem and Prickle?

Each one comes from a talisman: Tasty Bone for the wolf, Golem Kit for the golem, and Prickle's Mark for the Prickle. Talismans replace the passive artifacts from the first game.

Can companions be upgraded?

Fly Agaric Trousers make them deal 40% more damage and Woodsprite Crown makes them take 20% less. Their own base stats are not published.

Is this a good co-op build?

It is a fun secondary build. The pack adds damage with no effort, so you can support your team while they tank and finish.

Not confirmed yet for the Pack Leader Summoner Build

  • Companion damage, health and AI behavior are not published, so this build is graded on armor bonuses alone.
  • Whether Ground Heal works alongside summons or only on your kills is unclear.
