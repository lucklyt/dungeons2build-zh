How are the 1 to 5 scores worked out?

Each item in a loadout earns 1 point (a minor effect) or 2 points (a major effect) on the axes its listed effect touches. We add up the points for every equipped weapon, armor piece, artifact, talisman and enchantment, then turn the total into a score.

SurvivabilityEvade, deflect, reflect, healing over time, shields and max health.
MobilityExtra dodge charges, roll cooldown, speed boosts and movement bonuses.
Team supportBuffs and heals that reach allies, longer status effects and placed totems.

The scores describe what a loadout is built to do, not a measured result. They will change as item numbers are published, and the grade, which also weighs how much evidence exists, can change with them.

Where do these builds come from?

Two places. The first is what players recommended while playing early builds, mostly the Rocket and Horn pair, jump attacks and the co-op tools. The second is the item data itself: we looked for effects that stack, like three separate crit sources or armor that shortens artifact cooldowns. Nothing here is a stress-tested launch result, so treat grades as a starting point and check them against your own runs.

Build questions answered

What is the best build in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

跳劈暴击流 is the best build right now. It is graded S, the top grade on this page.

What is the best solo build?

跳劈暴击流 is the best solo pick, since its damage does not depend on teammates.

What is the best co-op build?

旗帜与篮子合作辅助 is the best co-op build, because its buffs and heals reach the whole party.

What is the best mage build?

Ranged Soul Caster Mage Build is the mage-style build. It is graded B because it needs a soul bar you keep filled and some of its artifact numbers are thin.

Is there a good summoner build?

Pack Leader Summoner Build is the summoner option. It is graded C because companion damage has no published numbers yet.

How are builds graded?

Each loadout is scored from 1 to 5 on five axes using the listed effects of its items, and the grade also weighs how much evidence exists. Both change as item numbers are published.
