Reflect Wall Tank

BGrade B tier · SolidTank

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

B-tier tank pick

The reflect wall tank soaks damage by making it hard to land: it evades melee, deflects projectiles and sends 100% of the damage you take back at the attacker. It is the sturdy pick for boss fights and co-op front lines.

What is the Reflect Wall Tank routine in a fight?

Immovable front line. You stand where the enemies are and let evasion, deflection and reflect do the work, with totems and a soul-fed heal filling the gaps.

  1. Place the totems firstPut Totem of Shielding between you and the ranged enemies, then drop Totem of Regeneration behind you. Both are placed effects, so plant them before the pull.
  2. Hit the pack and stand thereWalk into melee and stay put. Evade, reflect and deflect are passive, so your job is to be the thing they hit.
  3. Warding Chimes on big hitsUse the chimes when a boss winds up or a group commits. It toughens you, so time it to the biggest incoming damage.
  4. Every cooldown healsHealth Synergy turns each artifact use into regeneration, so rotate the three artifacts even when they are not strictly needed.
  5. Reset with a killIf a fight is going badly, kill something to try for Shadowcloak, and drink a potion to gain souls and heal at once.

Good and bad sides of the Reflect Wall Tank

Strengths

  • Three separate defensive layers: evade, deflect and reflect
  • Heals on kills, potions and artifact use
  • Protects a whole group with placed totems
  • Good against bosses that hit hard in melee

Weaknesses

  • No confirmed taunt, so enemies may attack your allies
  • Low damage output, so fights run long
  • Poison damage bypasses evade and deflect

How does the Reflect Wall Tank play with friends?

Front-line anchor. Take the boss's attention by standing closest and let teammates work from the side. Pairs well with a support player who can stack extra healing.

Player questions about the Reflect Wall Tank

Does Reflect actually return all damage?

The listed effect says 100% of damage taken is reflected at attackers. We have not confirmed whether that covers every damage type, so treat it as strong but unproven.

Can a tank pull enemy attention in co-op?

No taunt is confirmed. Position yourself as the nearest target and rely on passive defenses.

Is poison a problem for this build?

Yes. Players call it the most brutal thing in the early game, and evade and deflect do not stop it. Bring a potion.

Loose ends on the Reflect Wall Tank

  • The 100% reflect on Woodsprite Trunks is a listed launch-data value and has not been tested against bosses.
  • Warding Chimes' exact defensive value is not published.
