What does a fight look like with the Jump-Attack Crit Bruiser?

Front-line burst. You are always moving: jump, land, swing, roll, jump again. Slow weapons feel fast because the jump attack does the heavy lifting, and the whole kit is built to make those landings hit harder.

  1. Bunch the packWalk toward the group instead of chasing stragglers. A tight cluster is what a jump attack rewards, and a phantom-wing jump shot pulls the lazy ones in.
  2. Light the crit stackPop Fighter's Flute, then Warrior Drums a beat later. Both cost souls and go on a short cooldown, so glance at the soul bar before you commit.
  3. Jump, then hitJump and attack while airborne. Aim the landing at the biggest target: Pride of the Plains boosts the primary hit, and Crash Landing shoves everything else back.
  4. Roll out, roll inWhen something big winds up, roll away. Dynamo turns that roll into a charged next swing, and the amulet gets the roll back sooner.
  5. Finish the comboClose with the last hit of a normal combo so Fire Aspect sends its wave through anything you missed.
  6. Keep Death Cap for elitesSave the mushroom for the tough mob left standing. Faster swings mean more jump attacks per second.

What are the Jump-Attack Crit Bruiser's strengths and weak spots?

Strengths

  • Highest burst of any build we can assemble from listed gear
  • Three separate crit effects that stack cleanly
  • Jump attacks make slow weapons feel quick and hit several targets
  • Reflect and evade cover for the hits you take while diving in

Weaknesses

  • Blue empowered mobs shrug off melee, so switch to range there
  • Poison is brutal when you stand in the middle of a pack
  • Three artifacts all want souls, so a dry soul bar drops your damage

Does the Jump-Attack Crit Bruiser earn a spot in a party?

Front-line damage dealer. Stand in front, land the jump attacks and let teammates finish stragglers. It combines well with a Banner carrier, whose buff boosts melee attacks.

Common questions on the Jump-Attack Crit Bruiser

Is jump attack really better than normal attacks?

Yes. Players and developers both single it out: it hits multiple targets, knocks back, and works on slow weapons. One developer's advice for the demo was simply to spam it.

Do I need the Twisted Warden set for this build?

No. The blindfold and vest are the best matches, but the loop works with any armor. Farm the boss later and treat the set as an upgrade.

What should I change against blue empowered mobs?

Swap to your ranged weapon. Players report that melee barely scratches them, and range is the safe answer until you have better gear.

What is still unconfirmed on the Jump-Attack Crit Bruiser?

  • Fist of Iron lists no damage value in our data, so its contribution is unmeasured.
  • Whether Pride of the Plains counts a jump attack's landing as a melee hit is untested. We assume it does.
