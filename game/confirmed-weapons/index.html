All Weapons, Armor Sets and Artifacts in Minecraft Dungeons 2

Published September 25, 2026 · Updated September 29, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Minecraft Dungeons 2 launched with 48 melee and 18 ranged weapons, 31 armor sets, 37 artifacts, 20 talismans and 24 named enchantments. Mojang confirms 12 combat slots, four armor pieces and unlimited recharging ammo; item effects here come from launch-week catalog data.

Minecraft Dungeons II is out, and the gear catalog is now large enough to browse instead of guess at. Mojang states the rules of the system; the item names, numbers and effects below come from launch-week catalog data. Anything that catalog leaves blank is marked as such.

Checked in-game on October 1, 2026.

Every entry is also searchable in our gear database.

What does Mojang say about the gear system?

Four official statements frame everything else. Combat slots grow from six to twelve. Armor is four separate pieces, each able to roll random effects. Talismans are passive, and they level up as you use them. Ranged weapons have unlimited ammo that recharges on its own.

Artifacts are the active part: cooldown abilities that spend Souls, the resource earned from defeating enemies. Enchantment books go into a personal library, and you apply and level them at the Enchantsmith. Our enchanting guide walks through that loop.

Which weapon types are in the game?

Weapons come as 48 melee and 18 ranged types, plus unique variants of some of them. The base melee types are Axe, Battle Hammer, Battlestaff, Claws, Claymore, Clobberer, Cookiecutter, Double Daggers, Double Sickles, Gauntlets, Glaive, Greataxe, Greatsword, Mace, Mallet, Meat Cleaver, Pickaxe, Pike, Rapier, Riftslasher, Sabre, Scythe, Short Spear, Shovel, Spear, Sword, War Hammer.

The base ranged types are Bow, Crossbow (15 ammo), Dual Crossbows (20 ammo), Greatbow (10 ammo), Heavy Crossbow (8 ammo), Longbow, Powerbow, Rapid Crossbow, Scatter Crossbow (12 ammo), Shortbow (18 ammo), Trickbow (15 ammo). Where the catalog lists an ammo count, it is shown; a few bows have none listed. Each weapon type has its own jump attack, tied to the jump ability, so the weapon you carry changes what a jump does.

How do rarity tiers affect modifiers?

Rare gear carries one modifier, Special carries two, and Unique carries two with one of them fixed. A Blacksmith can raise gear power for emeralds, up to the Item Power cap for your Hero Level, and reroll one effect for echo shards. Rerolls are permanent and need Blacksmith level 1 for Rare, level 2 for Special and level 3 for Unique gear. Talismans cannot be upgraded there. Quick FAQ

Is there a full weapon list for Minecraft Dungeons 2? Yes, for names: 48 melee and 18 ranged. Damage numbers and drop rates are still missing.

Do talismans replace artifacts? No. Talismans are passive and level up with use. Artifacts are active, cost Souls and go on cooldown.

Which weapon is best? Nobody has published drop rates or damage values, so any ranking is a guess. Our builds tier list will cover it once the data allows.
