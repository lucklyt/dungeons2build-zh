Minecraft Dungeons 2 Co-op and Crossplay

Published September 24, 2026 · Updated October 1, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Minecraft Dungeons 2 supports up to four players: online co-op, split-screen couch co-op, or solo. Steam confirms cross-platform multiplayer, and Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo each require their own paid online subscription for online play.

Yes, Minecraft Dungeons II has crossplay, and up to four players can team up online, on one couch, or both at once. Console players need their platform’s paid online subscription, while PC players don’t.

Jump to: crossplay | per platform | party size | split-screen | joining friends | FAQ

Does Minecraft Dungeons 2 have crossplay?

Yes. Steam’s own store listing confirms “cross-platform multiplayer” as a supported feature, and the Xbox Store lists both “Xbox cross-platform multiplayer” and “Xbox cross-platform co-op.” The Nintendo Store also tags the game with “cross-platform play.” None of the four storefronts we checked publishes a platform-by-platform exclusion list, so PC, Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch players can all land in the same party, though no single store page spells out every pairing.

What does each platform need for online co-op?

It depends on your platform, and every console needs a paid subscription. The Xbox Store’s Deluxe Edition page states plainly: “Online multiplayer on console requires XBOX Game Pass Essential, Premium, or Ultimate (subscriptions sold separately).” The Nintendo Store lists “Online Play” under its Nintendo Switch Online feature callout, so Switch and Switch 2 players need an active membership. PlayStation’s store page notes “up to 4 online players” through the PS5’s standard online service, which on PS5 means PlayStation Plus. PC players on Steam or PC Game Pass aren’t gated behind an equivalent paid subscription on that platform.

How many players can play co-op?

Up to four. Every storefront we checked, Steam, Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo, lists a four-player cap for co-op, and the game is fully playable solo if you’d rather run the campaign alone. Co-op is an option layered on top of a complete single-player game, not a requirement to finish it.

Does it have split-screen couch co-op?

Yes. Steam’s store page lists “split-screen co-op” directly alongside online co-op as a supported feature, so local play is a distinct mode from playing online with friends. Mojang’s gameplay systems article separately describes local and online play as options within the same up-to-four-player structure.

Can two people share a console and still team up with friends online?

Yes. Mojang describes local and online co-op as compatible, so a couch pair and remote friends can join the same session. Neither Mojang nor any storefront has published how a split-screen pair appears to online-only players in the lobby, so expect it to work without relying on details we can’t source.

What changes for the party in co-op?

Emeralds you find are split between players automatically, and each player gets a mini inventory that doesn’t pause the game. Merchant levels and stock are per player, so every friend levels their own merchant and sees their own shelves. The session also scales as players join or leave mid-session.

Your hero type matters for online play. An online hero can take the same gear into other players’ worlds, while an offline hero stays offline except for couch co-op. How do I join a friend’s game?

Mojang’s gameplay-systems article describes matchmaking and party codes, so you can jump into a public session or share a code with friends. Steam also lists Remote Play Together.

Key takeaways

  • Crossplay is confirmed on the Steam, Xbox and Nintendo store pages, with no platform excluded.
  • The party cap is four players, and the campaign is fully playable solo.
  • Xbox needs Game Pass Essential, Premium or Ultimate, Switch needs Nintendo Switch Online, and PS5 needs PlayStation Plus. PC has no equivalent paid requirement.
  • Couch co-op and online play can be mixed in one session.
  • Emeralds are split between co-op players automatically, and merchant stock is per player.

Quick FAQ

Can Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and PC players all play together? Steam, Xbox and Nintendo store pages each independently confirm cross-platform play, and none lists a platform exclusion, so the four major platforms sharing a party is the confirmed picture, even without one page spelling out every pairing.

Do I need Xbox Game Pass just to play online on console? You need one of Xbox Game Pass Essential, Premium, or Ultimate for online multiplayer on an Xbox console specifically, per the Xbox Store’s own listing. Owning the game outright doesn’t remove that requirement on console.

Is split-screen the same as online co-op? No. The Steam store page lists them as separate supported features: split-screen co-op for local play on one screen, and online co-op, including cross-platform multiplayer, for playing with people elsewhere.

Do co-op players share emeralds? Emeralds found in co-op are split between players automatically, so nobody has to pick them up first.

Can I switch between solo and co-op mid-campaign? Mojang hasn’t published details on switching modes mid-save. The confirmed shape is that solo and co-op are both fully supported ways to play the campaign, and the session scales as players join or leave.

Not confirmed yet

None of the four store pages we checked names every platform pairing explicitly (for example, whether Switch and Switch 2 players share a crossplay pool with PS5 by default, or whether crossplay can be toggled off). Cross-progression, carrying one save between platforms, hasn’t been announced either. Exact local split-screen player counts and camera behavior (whether it’s a true split screen or a shared camera) also haven’t been detailed. We’ll update this page once Mojang or a platform holder publishes specifics after launch.
