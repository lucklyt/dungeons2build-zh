Minecraft Dungeons 2 Game Info

Straight answers about Minecraft Dungeons II: editions, platforms, co-op, Game Pass and PC requirements, checked against official sources.

All Game Info articles

  • All Minecraft Dungeons 2 Bosses: Attacks and Tells

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 has at least eight named bosses at launch. Attack details exist for the Twisted Warden, Copper Monstrosity, Sculk Monstrosity, Harmonizer and the final boss, Monstrous Opus. The Prime Enchanter is a named High Council member; two more have no attack data yet.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • All Weapons, Armor Sets and Artifacts in Minecraft Dungeons 2

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 launched with 48 melee and 18 ranged weapons, 31 armor sets, 37 artifacts, 20 talismans and 24 named enchantments. Mojang confirms 12 combat slots, four armor pieces and unlimited recharging ammo; item effects here come from launch-week catalog data.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • The Dog Companion Talisman: How It Fights and Respawns

    Minecraft Dungeons 2's bone talisman summons a dog companion that fights alongside you, draws enemy aggro, takes damage in your place, and respawns on a short timer if it goes down. It's a talisman, not an artifact, so it doesn't cost a cooldown to keep active.

    游戏信息Checked September 25, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 Release Time in Every Time Zone

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 is out. It unlocked at 08:00 UTC on September 29, 2026, one moment worldwide: 1 AM Pacific, 4 AM Eastern, 9 AM in the UK, 10 AM in Central Europe and 6 PM in Sydney. PlayStation Store data lists that time; no preload was offered.

    游戏信息Checked September 29, 2026

  • The Sift and Brave Haven: What Each One Actually Is

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 splits its setting in two: the Sift, a newly revealed dimension under illager invasion, and Brave Haven, the hub town where you meet the Blacksmith and Enchantsmith between runs. The story treats saving both as one shared goal, not two separate campaigns.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 Co-op and Crossplay

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 supports up to four players: online co-op, split-screen couch co-op, or solo. Steam confirms cross-platform multiplayer, and Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo each require their own paid online subscription for online play.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 vs 1: What Actually Changed

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 changes the original in six ways: a new Unreal Engine art style, a jump ability, four-piece armor instead of one slot, talismans for passive buffs, reworked enchanting, and an interconnected world with the Sift dimension and quests instead of repeated missions.

    游戏信息Checked September 24, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 版本, Prices and Deluxe Upgrade

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 has a 标准版 (29.99 EUR on Steam, 24.99 GBP on PlayStation) and a 豪华版 (49.99 EUR, 44.99 GBP). Deluxe adds two DLCs, four hero skins, the Soul Cape and the Blub pet. A separate Deluxe Upgrade exists, and pre-orders have closed.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 on Xbox Game Pass?

    Yes. Minecraft Dungeons 2 is confirmed day one on Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass by Xbox Wire and the Xbox 商店. It also supports Xbox Play Anywhere. Online play on console still needs its own Xbox Game Pass Essential, Premium or Ultimate subscription.

    游戏信息Checked September 25, 2026

  • What Minecraft Dungeons 2 Gameplay Is Actually Like

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 plays as an open-world action RPG: roam the map, enter dungeons and Sift rifts, fight with jump-enabled combat, and build around artifacts, talismans and four armor pieces. The story takes roughly nine hours, with Soul Storms after.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026

  • Is There a Minecraft Dungeons 2 Demo? What the Gamescom Preview Showed

    There is no public playable Minecraft Dungeons 2 demo you can download. Xbox and Mojang gave press a hands-on preview at Gamescom 2026 covering Brave Haven, minecart fast travel, the Blacksmith and Enchantsmith, loadout slots, the Copper Monstrosity boss, and real-time co-op inventory swaps.

    游戏信息Checked September 24, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 PC System Requirements

    Minimum PC specs: Core i3-8100 or Ryzen 3 2200G, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1050 or RX 560 with 2 GB VRAM. Recommended, confirmed on Steam's store page: Core i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1060 or RX 580 with 6 GB VRAM.

    游戏信息Checked September 25, 2026

  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 Review Scores: Is It Good?

    On launch day, September 29, 2026, Minecraft Dungeons 2 averaged 85 on one review aggregator and 75 on another, while Steam user reviews were Mixed at 49% positive from 1,227 reviews. Critics are warm; players are split over launch problems. All three numbers are still moving.

    游戏信息Checked September 29, 2026

  • The Minecraft Dungeons 2 Story, Explained

    Officially, Minecraft Dungeons 2 follows a hero on a quest to stop an emerging evil across two worlds, tied to Minecraft's Deep Dark and Ancient Cities. Mojang has confirmed the Twisted Warden and Copper Monstrosity as bosses but hasn't named a main villain yet.

    游戏信息Checked September 24, 2026

  • Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 Worth It? Post-Launch Verdict

    Launch-week verdict: Minecraft Dungeons 2 is worth $29.99 for loot-chasers and co-op groups, since the story runs about 9 hours and the postgame has Soul Storms and Uniques. Wait or use Game Pass if you want a long solo campaign.

    游戏信息Checked October 1, 2026
