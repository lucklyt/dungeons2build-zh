Minecraft Dungeons 2 Release Time in Every Time Zone

Published September 25, 2026 · Updated September 29, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Minecraft Dungeons 2 is out. It unlocked at 08:00 UTC on September 29, 2026, one moment worldwide: 1 AM Pacific, 4 AM Eastern, 9 AM in the UK, 10 AM in Central Europe and 6 PM in Sydney. PlayStation Store data lists that time; no preload was offered.

Minecraft Dungeons 2 is live. It did not stagger its launch by region: the PlayStation Store’s listing gives one worldwide unlock moment, and every platform shares the September 29, 2026 release date. Mojang’s launch post went up the same day.

Want the time in your own zone? The table below lists the main regions; for anywhere else, convert 08:00 UTC to your local time.

What time did Minecraft Dungeons 2 unlock?

It unlocked at 08:00 UTC on September 29, 2026. That comes from the PlayStation Store’s release metadata, which lists 2026-09-29T08:00:00Z and shows pre-orders as closed. No written announcement states the hour, so this is store data rather than a quoted statement from Mojang.

What time was that where I live?

Here is 08:00 UTC on September 29, 2026 converted to common timezones. On that day the US, UK and Europe were still on summer time, and Sydney had not yet moved to daylight saving, so these are the offsets in effect.

Did the unlock time vary by platform?

Not according to any storefront. The Xbox and PlayStation listings share the same release date and do not carve out separate windows for console, PC or cloud. If your store app showed a different time on launch day, trust that in-app countdown over this page.

Was there a preload, and how big is the download?

Press coverage says no preload was offered. Download sizes are on the store pages now: Nintendo’s store shows roughly 6.8 GB on the first Switch and 6 GB on Switch 2. Other platforms did not publish a figure on the pages we checked.

An older listing shows a smaller Switch figure, but the store page is more recent, so we use Nintendo’s number.

Should I have pre-ordered?

Pre-orders are closed, and they never changed the unlock time. They added cosmetic bonuses only. See our editions and pricing breakdown for what each edition includes, and our beginner tips for your first hour in the game.

Quick FAQ

Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 out worldwide at the same time? Yes, at one global UTC moment rather than a staggered regional rollout.

What if my country is not in the table? Convert 08:00 UTC on September 29, 2026 with any timezone converter.

Is it on Game Pass? Yes. It arrived on Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass on launch day, with no separate Game Pass unlock time published.

Is the game working smoothly? Some PC players report account-linking problems and crashes; see our review scores page for the details.

Not confirmed yet

No official day-one patch notes or known-issues list has been published. Download sizes for Xbox, PlayStation and PC are not on the pages we checked. We will add them once a platform lists them.
