How long is Minecraft Dungeons 2?

The main story takes about 9 hours at most, according to a player who finished it on launch day. That puts it in the same range as the first game, and short for a $30 title if you only want a campaign.

Side quests, secret chests and clearing each area to 100% stretch that a lot. Players who explore everything report far longer playtimes, but that is optional time, not required story.

Is there enough endgame?

For loot players, yes, and it is the main reason to buy. The postgame is built around Soul Storms, a rotating event where you destroy three generators and open reward chests, plus Unique gear that drops more often near endgame and in Storm areas.

Other things to do after the credits:

  • Rematch bosses: use a boss’s Boss Totem to replay it for additional rewards.
  • Reroll gear abilities with echo shards, which cap at 100. Players say mid-game you are capped all the time, so rerolling is where the shards go.
  • Revisit early side quests, which players say are noticeably tougher after the story.
  • Tune difficulty yourself. There are no threat levels now, only Easy, Normal and Hard, which you can change in game.

The catch: fewer difficulty tiers means less to chase than a threat-level ladder. If your favorite part of the first game was climbing difficulty, check the notes under “Not confirmed yet” below.

What do players like and dislike so far?

Critics are warm on the game, while Steam players are split mostly over launch stability. Our review scores page tracks the numbers.

What players like

  • Swapping builds is quick: players compare it to changing your shoes, so don’t get attached to early gear.
  • Enchantments live in a personal library, so disenchanting never loses you the weapon. Players say go wild and enchant everything.
  • The jump hides secret chests and adds jump attacks. If it looks climbable, follow it.
  • The open map is full of side quests that reward Unique gear, with minecart fast travel between stations.
  • Four-piece armor and talismans give you real choices. A pet wolf talisman is called nearly essential for solo play.

What players dislike

  • Rerolling feels very RNG. One player burned over 100 shards and got a worse roll.
  • The merchant is widely called poor value, and its stock only refreshes every 30 minutes unless you pay 3 echo shards.
  • The 100 echo shard cap annoys farmers who want to bank more.
  • PC players report login failures and crashes on launch.
  • Co-op gets visually busy with four players and a boss on screen.

Is it worth it solo or only in co-op?

It works solo, but co-op is where it shines. Solo players say you can feel underpowered against ranged enemies, and a companion talisman makes a big difference. In co-op you get a mini inventory that doesn’t pause the game, and emeralds you find are split between players automatically.

If you mostly play alone, try Game Pass before paying. If you have a regular group, the $29.99 split across a few friends is easy to justify. Crossplay details are on our co-op page.

Is it worth full price or should you wait for a sale or Game Pass?

At $29.99 Standard it is already priced under most 2026 releases, so there is little pressure to wait unless you are unsure. The Deluxe Edition is $49.99 and mostly adds cosmetics and DLC, so only buy it if you want those. Our editions breakdown lists exactly what each one includes.

Do this:

  1. On Game Pass Ultimate or PC Game Pass: play it free and buy later only if you want to keep it.
  2. Not on Game Pass and sure you like loot games: buy Standard now.
  3. Not sure, or on PC and nervous about bugs: wait a week or two and watch the Steam score.

Is it better than Dungeons 1?

Most players treat it as a bigger, more build-focused sequel: an open map, four armor slots and a deeper loot layer. Whether that beats the 2020 game depends on how much you value those systems, so see our Dungeons 2 vs 1 comparison.

Quick FAQ

Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 worth buying right now? Yes for loot-chasers and co-op groups. Wait if you want a long solo story or are worried about PC launch bugs.

How long is the main story? About 9 hours at most, per a launch-day finisher.

Do I need Dungeons 1 first? No, it is a standalone story.

Is the merchant worth spending on? Players mostly say no, apart from early upgrades. Keep your emeralds and use drops first. The merchant never sells Uniques.
