Rainy Plains, in short

Rainy Plains is the sixth region of Minecraft Dungeons 2, a muddy stretch of grassland with Puddle Pond Village as its base and the Supreme Evoker's Tower at the far end. The tower holds a two-phase boss, the Copper Monstrosity, which is the reason most players come here.

Tips from people who cleared Rainy Plains

  • Learn the Copper Monstrosity's wipe attacks before you commit. Many bosses here have moves that can take a whole health bar.
  • Farm the area that drops the item you want. The map's area info lists each region's drops.
  • Break the fight into its two phases and save your artifacts for the second one.

Rainy Plains 常见问题

Where is the Copper Monstrosity?

In the Supreme Evoker's Tower in Rainy Plains. You reach it during the Wading Rainy Plains quest, right after finding the Ichor Core in the Red Tower.

Does the Supreme Evoker's Tower have a minecart station?

No, none are listed. Puddle Pond is the nearest fast-travel point.

What should I bring to the tower?

A build that can dodge and heal. The tank and the roll-and-shoot kiter both fit the boss's big area attacks.

Not confirmed yet in Rainy Plains

  • Drop tables for the Copper Monstrosity are unknown.
  • The exact mob list for the Supreme Evoker's Tower is not published beyond the boss's minions.
