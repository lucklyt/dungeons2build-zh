Squid Coast, in short

Squid Coast is the first region in Minecraft Dungeons 2. It is a short tutorial stretch on the Overworld: Illagers attack the camp, you clear enemies so villagers can reach the boats, and the whole group sails to Brave Haven, where the real hub begins.

What do players tell you to do in Squid Coast?

  • Use the guidance line that points at your objective. You can ignore it, and it sometimes switches on by itself when a quest starts.
  • Sculk is corrupting the land here, which is the story reason the camp is abandoned rather than defended.
  • Nothing on this coast needs a build. Save your first item choices for Brave Haven and Honeycomb Fields.

Quick answers on Squid Coast

What happens at Squid Coast?

Illagers raid the camp, sculk eats the blocks around it, and you protect the villagers while they board boats. The chapter ends when you arrive in Brave Haven.

Is Squid Coast the same as Brave Haven?

No. Squid Coast is the starting region and Brave Haven is the town you sail to. Fast travel and the merchants live in Brave Haven.

Can you skip Squid Coast?

No. It is the first step of the campaign order and the game starts here.

Not confirmed yet in Squid Coast

  • Squid Coast has little published beyond its name, so its mob list and loot are not documented.
  • Whether you can return to Squid Coast after the boat trip, and what it holds later, is unknown.
