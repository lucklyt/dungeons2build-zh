Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Frozen Highlands, in short

Frozen Highlands is the seventh region of Minecraft Dungeons 2 and the last Overworld stop before the Sift. It is a cold, connected stretch with an Illager Fortress and a Frozen Fortress inside it, a secret Illager code to crack and patrols of pillagers, vindicators and royal guards.

How do players handle Frozen Highlands?

  • This is your last Overworld stop, so clear a few side quests for loot before the Sift. The in-game map shows what the area gives.
  • Group your artifacts by role before you leave: the Sift makes water and ichor hurt, and you want a heal or a shield ready.
  • Illagers patrol in mixed groups here. A horn or knockdown artifact splits them up nicely.

Common questions about Frozen Highlands

What comes after Frozen Highlands?

The campaign moves into the Sift, starting with Singer's Meadow, followed by Humbler Huskland and Lullaby Hills.

Which mobs live in Frozen Highlands?

Pillagers, vindicators, royal guards, creepers and neutral goats are listed for the region.

Is there a boss in Frozen Highlands?

None is confirmed. Boss that guard the story around it sit in Rainy Plains and the Deep Dark.

Not confirmed yet in Frozen Highlands

  • No boss is listed for Frozen Highlands, although the Illager Fortress may hold one.
  • Both fortresses have only stub entries, so loot and layout are unknown.
  • How the secret Illager code works has not been documented.
