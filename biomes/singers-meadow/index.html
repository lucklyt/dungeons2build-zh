Singer's Meadow, in short

Singer's Meadow is the first region of the Sift in Minecraft Dungeons 2 and the eighth stop in the campaign. It is a lush, vertical meadow where you wake four silenced echo golems, reactivate the note blocks, fight the Sculk Monstrosity and open the Musical Gate to Humbler Huskland.

What do players tell you to do in Singer's Meadow?

  • Sift water hurts, and stepping in ichor costs health and souls at once. Players call that double bad, so route around it.
  • Shooting an arrow at an enemy projectile can cancel it, which helps against the monstrosity's barrage.
  • The vertical terrain makes jumping useful. Jump attacks and jump shots both work for reaching golem sites.

Quick answers on Singer's Meadow

What is the Lost Harmonies quest?

You visit four silenced echo golems around Singer's Meadow. Each has a marker on the map, so you walk up and interact with it.

Where is the Sculk Monstrosity?

In Singer's Meadow, as part of the A Monstrosity in the Meadow questline. Pass the Musical Gate afterward to reach Humbler Huskland.

Is the water in the Sift dangerous?

Yes. Sift water and ichor both hurt because they are soul-filled, and ichor also drains your souls.

Not confirmed yet in Singer's Meadow

  • Sift mobs are listed for the whole dimension, not per region, so the mob table mixes Sift-wide entries.
  • A three-state Sift tide system is reported by players but is not confirmed on official pages.
  • Whether the Meadow contains dungeons or rift events beyond the quest areas is unknown.
