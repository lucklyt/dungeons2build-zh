Honeycomb Fields in Minecraft Dungeons 2

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 29, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Honeycomb Fields, in short

Honeycomb Fields is the second region in Minecraft Dungeons 2 and the first place the game opens up. Farm and beehive quests fill it, a Grand Illusioner rift releases the first tanky blue mobs, and the Creeper Candle quest pays out a placeable bomb artifact.

What do players tell you to do in Honeycomb Fields?

  • Pick up quests before you wander. A quest's target area is usually empty until you have accepted it, so exploring first wastes the trip.
  • Use ranged weapons on blue mobs and keep your distance. A group of four found the only way to hurt them was with arrows and rockets.
  • The Honey artifact releases bees that also slow an Enderman, which is handy if you meet one.
  • Spend emeralds as you find them. The cap is 9999 and anything over it is lost.

Quick answers on Honeycomb Fields

What are the blue mobs in Honeycomb Fields?

They are soul-enchanted or empowered variants released by the Grand Illusioner's rift. They are much tougher than normal mobs and drop echo shards.

How do I get the Creeper Candle artifact?

Finish the creeper candle quest here. It rewards an artifact that places lit candles which detonate after a short fuse.

Which build should I bring?

Anything with a solid ranged option. The Rocket and Horn crowd controller is a common early pick for the empowered mobs.
