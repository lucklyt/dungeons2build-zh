Best Artifacts in Minecraft Dungeons 2 (Early Game and Beyond)

Published September 29, 2026 · Updated October 1, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

The best artifacts in Minecraft Dungeons 2 are Death Cap Mushroom, Fighter's Flute and Firework Arrow, with Humbling Horn as the early partner. You get three artifact slots, and the second opens after the lost-merchant quest.

Which artifacts should you use early on?

Artifacts are the active abilities. They spend souls and sit on a cooldown, unlike talismans, which stay on all the time. Players keep reaching for the rocket, the horn, a basket or banner in co-op, and Creeper Candle from a quest.

Firework Arrow is the one everybody mentions. Pair it with the horn: the horn knocks the pack down, and rockets land while they are stuck. Both work at range, which matters against the blue empowered mobs that shrug off melee. Our poison and empowered mobs guide explains why range wins those fights.

Equip the rocket in your first slot and fire it from the bow. Arrows recharge on their own, and a starter bow holds 15, so you are not rationing shots.

When does the second artifact slot unlock?

After the lost-merchant quest in Howling Woods. Until then you have one slot, and the rocket sits there fine. Players put the second slot around hero level 5, and the campaign walkthrough shows where the quest falls in the region order. The third slot is reported at level 10.

Pairings work because one artifact sets up the next, or because two buffs share a window.

For artifact-heavy kits, armor matters as much as the artifacts. Oracle Tights give 30% shorter artifact cooldowns and Mad Sifter Vest gives 25% more artifact damage, and our armor sets guide covers both.

Which combos are worth trying early?

  • Rocket and Horn is the default recommendation.
  • Banner with a melee weapon strengthens friends’ swings, so it suits a tank in the group.
  • Honey Dipper with anything ranged: the bees slow a target while you shoot it.
  • Basket before a hard boss phase, not during it. Players call its 15s cooldown slow, which is why it sits at C tier.

For weapon pairings, the build plans guide has three shapes to start from.

Does TNT hurt your teammates?

Yes. TNT is the one source of friendly fire in the game, according to a developer, so throw it when friends are out of the blast. Groups call “fire in the hole” before each throw. If you are new to a party, hold TNT until you know where everyone stands.

Quick checklist for your first hour

  1. Equip Firework Arrow first.
  2. Finish the lost-merchant quest for slot two.
  3. Add Humbling Horn as your second artifact.
  4. Swap in the Basket or Banner once you group up.

Key takeaways

  • You get three artifact slots: one early, the second after the lost-merchant quest.
  • Death Cap Mushroom costs 20 souls on a 4s cooldown and sits in S tier.
  • Fighter’s Flute and Warrior Drums each cost 45 souls on a 5s cooldown.
  • Totem of Regeneration is the priciest at 65 souls and a 27s cooldown.
  • Picnic Basket costs 15 souls with a 15s cooldown, the longest wait of the cheap picks.
  • Artifacts count toward item power, so an empty slot costs you.

Quick FAQ

Which artifact is strongest? Death Cap Mushroom and Fighter’s Flute lead our tier list. Firework Arrow is the safe early pick.

How many artifacts can you equip? Three. They sit apart from talisman and armor slots.

Do artifacts have cooldowns? Yes, and each costs souls. Soul Reaper and Alchemist Loafers help keep the soul bar full.

  • Echo Ocarina (40 souls, 10s) comes from a press guide and has no listed effect yet.

  • TNT is not in our item data, so its cost and cooldown are unknown.
  • Third slot level (10) is a player report.
  • Humbling Horn’s scaling by level has not been documented.
