Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 Open World?

Published September 24, 2026 · Updated September 30, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Sep 30, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

No, not a traditional open world. Minecraft Dungeons 2 is one connected Overworld plus a second dimension, the Sift, and the in-game map screen gives each its own tab.

No, Minecraft Dungeons 2 is not a traditional open world. It is one connected Overworld you can walk end to end, plus a second dimension called the Sift, and the map screen gives each of them its own tab. You follow a story, but you travel on foot and by minecart instead of picking missions from a menu.

This page covers what the map screen shows, every region in campaign order, how fast travel works and what is still unclear.

Why doesn’t Mojang call it open world?

Mojang avoids the label on purpose. Xbox Wire describes “a contiguous, fixed and interconnected world, dotted with dungeons.” You still follow a story with objectives, so you are not dropped into a map and told to go anywhere. What changed from the first game is how you reach those objectives: you walk between them instead of returning to camp and choosing the next level from a table.

What does the map screen show?

The map has two tabs, Overworld and The Sift. Each area carries a “% Items Collected” counter, and a “View Area Items” button lists what you have not found yet in that area. A Recenter button snaps the view back to you, and a Minecart Stations shortcut jumps between your stations.

Active Soul Storms show up on the map with a countdown timer and the area they are in. The map is also where you check which area drops the gear you want before you go there.

What regions are on the map?

West to east, the Overworld runs from Brave Haven, the hub village in the far west, through Honeycomb Fields and Howling Woods to Frozen Highlands. Rainy Plains sits to the south. Villages with vendors are spread through the regions, so you do not have to run back to Brave Haven for every shop.

Is there fast travel in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

Yes. Minecart Stations are the fast travel points. The map has a Minecart Stations shortcut that jumps you between them, so you do not have to walk back the long way. Haven Station is the first one you unlock (see the campaign walkthrough). Between stations you are on foot, and jumping gives you ledges and gaps to cross that the first game could not ask for.

How does jumping change exploration?

Jumping was the feature fans asked for most, according to executive producer Andreas Skoglund. Xbox Wire says it enhances the possibilities for exploration, and it is what gives the series real verticality for the first time. Expect ledges, gaps and platforms you cross by hand instead of walking around.

What side content is out there?

Doorways scattered through the world lead into randomly generated dungeons separate from the main story. Clearing them and defeating soul-empowered mobs both pay echo shards, which level merchants and pay for enchanting. The “View Area Items” list tells you what is left in each area, so you can tell when an area is finished.

If you lose your bearings, a guiding line points you back to your objective with a single button press. Gear questions go to the gear database.

How does the Sift differ from the Overworld?

The Sift is a second dimension you reach partway through the story, and its terrain and enemies look nothing like the Overworld. A portal icon on the map marks the link between the two. The map treats it as its own world, which is why it gets a separate tab. For the plot side, read the Sift and Brave Haven explainer and the story guide.

Quick FAQ

Is Minecraft Dungeons 2 open world like Zelda or Elden Ring? No. It is one connected world with a story-driven route, and Mojang avoids the label.

Can I see what I have missed in an area? Yes. Each area shows a “% Items Collected” counter, and “View Area Items” lists what is left.

Does exploration work in co-op? Yes. Up to four players can explore the same world together, see the co-op and crossplay guide.

Can I go back to earlier regions? Yes. Minecart Stations let you jump back, and the campaign itself returns you to Howling Woods.

Not confirmed yet

Mojang has not published an exact map size or a size comparison with the first game, so “bigger” is a structural claim, not a number. We also have not confirmed what happens to free roaming after the ending. Some named locations (Ancient City, Echo Den, The Carapace) have no campaign step yet. We will add details once we have tested them in-game.
