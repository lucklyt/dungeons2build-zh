Minecraft Dungeons 2 Beginner Tips: 15 Things I Wish I Knew

Published September 24, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Fifteen practical Minecraft Dungeons 2 tips from launch week: jump to find chests, dismantle without fear, spend capped shards on re-rolls, rematch bosses at their Boss Totem, read the Sift states and leave areas at about 60%.

The best Minecraft Dungeons 2 tips are the ones that save you time and shards: jump at anything climbable, dismantle gear without fear, rematch bosses for extra rewards, and leave an area before you have emptied it. These are the things I wish I knew before playing, collected from launch-week player reports. Anything only one player claims sits at the end under “Not confirmed yet”.

How do I find secret chests in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

  1. Jump at everything that looks climbable. Any spire, rock or rooftop you can reach is worth a hop. Players say chests often only pop up once you climb a spire, hop a ridge or get close. Check villages top to bottom, because one chest appeared on the very last jump of a player’s run.
  2. Chests and dungeons come back. Players report they refresh on login or daily, so log in and run the route again. Brave Haven alone hides at least five chests, and several sit on the small island that is easy to miss. The open-world guide covers the wider map.
  3. Check the map for dungeon and rift entrances. If a dungeon door is closed, look around for another entrance and come back later.
  4. Golden doors are alcoves. If there is no big dungeon gateway, a golden door leads to a small alcove with a short ambush and decent loot. These are always there, unlike the longer dungeons that rotate.

What should I do with my gear, echo shards and enchantments?

  1. Dismantling does not destroy anything. Dismantle or disenchant and you get the weapon or armor back, the enchantment book back and your enchantment points back. You only lose a small amount of echo shards, so do not panic about trying things. Our enchanting guide walks through the Enchantsmith.
  2. Spend capped echo shards on re-rolls. Shards cap at 100, and mid-game you will sit at the cap. Early on they are scarce, so hold them. Later, re-roll the random abilities on rare and special gear. Re-rolls need Blacksmith level 1 for Rare gear, level 2 for Special and level 3 for Unique, and they are permanent, so set a budget before you start.
  3. Learn what each rarity can do. Common gear has no abilities, rare has one, special has two. Unique gear has a fixed special ability plus one or two extras you can re-roll, and common or rare gear cannot take extra abilities. Save re-rolls for gear that can keep them. The Blacksmith’s power upgrades raise an item up to the Item Power cap for your Hero Level, but they never work on talismans.
  4. Do not get attached to early gear. Better pieces keep dropping from enemies, missions and side quests. Dismantle spares for emeralds, spend at the merchant only when a piece is clearly underleveled, and keep your gear within about ten levels of your power level. The power level guide explains why. Our farming guide covers shards and emeralds in more depth.
  5. Enchant everything. Books you find go into a personal library that you keep permanently. Players say to go nuts, and they are right: Somersault, which chains several rolls, gets singled out as especially useful.
  6. Test your weapons on a dummy. Hammers do not always hit an area, and some jump attacks deal less than a normal swing. Their real job is stun and knockback. Find a practice dummy in a village before you commit a build to a weapon. The loot and gear guide lists what to look for on drops.

How do I get more Unique gear?

  1. Rematch bosses for extra rewards. Once you have beaten a boss, go back to its area and use its Boss Totem to fight it again for additional rewards. Bosses scale up as you level, so go in with real gear.
  2. Leave an area at about 60% and return for Storms. Once you have found about 55 to 60% of an area’s loot, move on. Soul Storms add new Uniques, and some items only appear after the main story. Our Soul Storm guide has the details. Talismans also fill a big part of the late game, and the secret talismans guide lists where to look.
  3. Check the Sift state in the top right. The Sift runs in three states. Flow is normal. Endure stops your souls from regenerating and makes enemies tougher. Thrive makes souls come in faster and artifacts recharge sooner, and it lasts until you move to the next Sift. Sift excursions and rifts are also your best source of enchantment books.

Is there anything to know about travel and co-op?

  1. Find the minecart stations. They are not always obvious. Check corners near boss areas, then use them to zip between unlocked fast travel points from the world map.
  2. Co-op shares emeralds automatically. Each player gets a mini inventory that does not pause the game, and emeralds are split between you for free. That makes four-player runs a good way to learn the map. Our co-op and crossplay guide covers party setup, and our build plans guide helps a group pick roles.

Quick FAQ

What is the best tip for a new Minecraft Dungeons 2 player? Jump at everything that looks climbable and dismantle freely. Both cost you almost nothing and reveal loot you would otherwise miss.

Do I lose my weapon if I disenchant it? No. You get the weapon, the enchantment book and your enchantment points back. You only lose a small amount of echo shards.

Should I save echo shards? Early, yes, because they are scarce. Once you reach the 100 cap, spend them on re-rolls.

How do I fight a boss again? Return to the boss’s area after you defeat it and use its Boss Totem to replay it for additional rewards.

Not confirmed yet

  • The re-roll cost steps of 1, 2 and 3 shards come from a single player’s run.
  • Whether a boss rematch always drops a Unique, or only the first time, is reported by one player.
  • Whether dungeon and rift entrances change per login or per day is reported by one player.
  • Whether the higher-rarity perk (Raider) acts on chests and the extra-drops perk (Looter) on killed enemies is still unclear. Equip loot perks before you open chests either way.
