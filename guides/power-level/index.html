How Power Level and Item Power Work, and How to Level Up Fast

Published October 1, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Hero level comes from XP, item power comes from your gear. Empty slots drag item power down, so fill every slot, run the early Honeycomb Fields quests, and use the merchant refresh loop to turn emeralds into power.

To level up fast in Minecraft Dungeons 2, finish the five early Honeycomb Fields quests, which took one player from item power 3 to 6 and hero level 4 to 6, then fill every gear slot and run the merchant refresh loop. Hero level comes from XP and item power comes from your gear, so the two move separately. Numbers below are from launch-week play, and the last section lists what is still unconfirmed.

What is the difference between power level and item power?

Your inventory shows a Level (hero level) and an Item Power, plus four small numbers: melee, ranged, armor and artifacts. Item power is built from those four. Players report one enchantment point per level gained, so points track hero level.

An empty slot drags its whole group down. In one run, a level 5 hero had item power 3 and armor 2 because the chest slot was empty. Swapping level 2 leggings for level 6 Beekeeper leggings raised armor from 2 to 3 and item power from 3 to 4. Fill every slot, even with weak gear.

When do artifact slots unlock?

The first artifact slot is open early, the second unlocks at hero level 5, and the third at level 10. Artifacts count toward item power, so an empty artifact slot hurts the same way an empty armor slot does.

Artifacts cost souls and often have cooldowns. The Totem of Shielding costs 30 souls with a 15 second cooldown, and the Creeper Candle costs 25 souls with a 5 second cooldown. See early game artifacts for picks.

How do you level up fast in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

The quickest early route is this order in Honeycomb Fields, which players report took item power from 3 to 6 without grinding:

  1. Finish Escape from Squid Coast. Brave Haven opens as your hub, and Haven Station gives you fast travel.
  2. Do The Illagers from the Rift. It takes you through your first rift into Singer’s Meadow. Clear the ambush and return.
  3. Take Keeper of the Bees. Collect planks, pick a build spot away from sculk, clear monsters and build the hive. You get the Hivemind Hardhat, which makes your positive statuses last 70% longer.
  4. Take The Creeper Candle Conspiracy. Follow the glowing trail into the cave, clear the ambush waves, and read the banner on the soul-corrupted pillager before you charge in. You get the Creeper Candle plus armor pieces.
  5. Finish The Lost Merchant to unlock the merchant and open the bridge to Howling Woods.

Smash every pot you pass for emeralds, and use the map’s “view area items” button to see what you missed. The campaign walkthrough covers what comes next.

How does the merchant raise item power?

The merchant stocks 3 items per rarity in each category: armor, melee weapons, ranged weapons and artifacts. Emeralds buy items and echo shards level the merchant. Level 1 shows only common gear. Reaching level 2 costs 10 echo shards and opens rare, and level 3 costs 15 and opens special. The merchant never sells Uniques.

Players rate ten shards on a merchant level above three shards on an early refresh. Stock refreshes automatically every 30 minutes, and a manual Refresh Now costs 3 echo shards.

The loop works like this:

  1. Spend emeralds on the best upgrade for an empty or weak slot.
  2. Refresh (free every 30 minutes, 3 shards on demand), compare the new stock with the compare button, and buy again.
  3. Salvage the old gear for emeralds with the multi-salvage button.

One run went from item power 5 to 6 without fighting and ended with only 75 emeralds left. Emeralds in your pocket do not raise item power, so spend them. Later the merchant is poor value for long-term gear, but it is great for filling gaps early.

Does the Blacksmith keep gear at your power level?

Yes. Upgrading at the Blacksmith raises a piece’s Item Power up to the cap set by your Hero Level, and it never works on talismans. Cost rises as the gap grows, so players suggest keeping gear within about 10 levels of you. Re-rolling abilities needs Blacksmith level 1 for Rare gear, level 2 for Special and level 3 for Unique, and rerolls are permanent. Common gear has no abilities, rare has one and special has two, so re-rolls only matter on rare and above. The Blacksmith unlocks during the quest The Missing Note Blocks. The enchanting guide covers the Enchantsmith costs.

How do you get XP quickly later on?

Re-fight bosses for the best hero XP, and use dungeons, rifts and Soul Storms for shards and gear. The farming guide has the full routes, and the boss guide has attack patterns.

Quick FAQ

What is item power? A rating from your gear across melee, ranged, armor and artifacts.

Why did my item power not change after leveling? Hero level and item power are separate. Gear drives item power.

How many artifact slots do I get? Three: one early, one at level 5 and one at level 10.

Is the merchant worth it? Early, yes. Later, players call it poor value.
