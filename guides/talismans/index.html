Minecraft Dungeons 2 has 24 talismans, and the right picks depend on your build: 犰狳护符 for any roll-heavy play, 铁拳 for melee damage, and 蜂蜡印记 when you need more health. Every talisman is passive and levels up to 3 the longer you wear it.

护符如何升级？

Wearing a talisman levels it. 护符s level through XP gained while equipped, and level 3 is the cap. Level 1 to 2 takes 18,480 XP, and level 2 to 3 takes 73,920 XP, for 92,400 XP in total. Swap fresh talismans in so they can catch up. Merchants do not sell levels.

Scaling shows in the numbers. 铁拳 goes 10%, 20%, then 35% more melee damage. 蜂蜡印记 goes from 20% to 35% more max health, and 搜刮者护符 from 1% to 4% extra drops. Early on a talisman you just found is weaker than the one you have worn for hours, so give a promising one time before judging it.

护符栏位如何运作？

护符s have their own slots, separate from the three artifact slots and from armor. Players report three talisman slots, and the starter talisman adds 20% max health. Bonuses you gain also apply to your pets, according to players who run companions.

目前凑齐配套护甲还没有套装加成，所以护符选择是你专精的地方。填满每个栏位，哪怕只是一般的东西。空栏位比弱护符更拉低你的物品威力。

哪些护符适合哪种配装？

每种用途选一个护符，而不是叠加几乎重复的。

哪些护符适合伤害流？

铁拳 adds up to 35% melee damage and stacks with other damage buffs, so it suits every melee build. 节俭吊坠 gives up to 25% more damage as your emeralds climb toward 7000, which pays off if you stockpile. 豹猫之爪 boosts jump attacks, the move our 跳跃攻击斗士 配装所围绕的招式。

哪些护符适合坦克？

犰狳护符 cuts roll cooldown by 20%, and rolling is your main defense. Add 治愈之心 for 2% health per second after five quiet seconds, and 蜂蜡印记 for max health. 傀儡套件 summons a golem that shields you, and the 反弹墙坦克 中。

哪些护符适合辅助流？

幽匿徽章 extends positive statuses by 15%, which matters when your buffs go to the whole party. 治愈之心 and 蜂蜡印记 keep the healer standing. The 旗帜与篮筐辅助配装 用上了全部三种。

哪些护符适合召唤流？

美味骨头 summons a wolf, 傀儡套件 a golem and 荆棘印记 the Prickle companion. Companion gear such as Fly Agaric Trousers (+40% companion damage) multiplies them. Our 狗狗伙伴攻略 讲解了狼如何作战， 首领配装 叠加了全部三种召唤。

哪些护符适合法器和灵魂流？

灵魂碎片 adds 15% more Soul capacity, and 扭曲之牙 raises souls gained. Players report 扭曲之牙 winning for artifact spam. 高效精华 only trims artifact cooldown by 4%, so only take it if a slot is free.

哪些护符适合战利品流？

幸运四叶草 and 搜刮者护符 raise drop chances, but the numbers are small. 幸运四叶草 gives 2%, 4% and 7% by level. Our 战利品装备攻略 说明了更大的战利品收益来自哪里。

要点速览

  • 共有 24 个护符，全部被动，上限均为 3 级。
  • 犰狳护符 is the only S-tier talisman in our list, for its 20% roll cooldown cut.
  • 铁拳 scales from 10% to 35% melee damage across its three levels.
  • 蜂蜡印记 scales from 20% to 35% more max health.
  • 幸运四叶草 is rated D: 2% to 7% bonus loot loses to anything that helps you survive.
  • 护符s need 18,480 XP for level 2 and 92,400 XP in total for level 3.
  • 八个护符位于隐藏地点，第九个由一扇锁着的门后的首领掉落。

快速问答

哪个护符最强？ 犰狳护符 for most loadouts, since rolling keeps you alive. Melee players pair it with 铁拳.

如何给护符升级？ 佩戴即可。每个护符在装备时获得经验，最高 3 级，总共需要 92,400 经验。

能从商人那里获得护符吗？ 玩家反馈它们来自支线任务、后期剧情任务和隐藏宝箱，而非商店。

隐藏护符在哪里？ 上方的攻略列出了全部 9 个的位置以及苔藓巨兽首领战。

尚未确认

  • 荧石瓶: our data says potion cooldown is 35% shorter at tier III, while one player reported 25%.
  • 扭曲之牙: the exact souls bonus is not listed. One player reported 140%.
  • 豹猫之爪, 荆棘印记 and 铁拳’s level-by-level numbers are only partly published.
  • 蜂蜡印记 tier II is not listed.
  • 护符 slot count (three) comes from player reports.
  • 摇摆石 has no effect listed in our data.
  • 大多数护符的掉落地点没有记录。
