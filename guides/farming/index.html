How to Farm XP, Echo Shards and Uniques in Minecraft Dungeons 2

Published September 30, 2026

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

Short answer

Re-fight bosses for XP, clear dungeons and rifts for echo shards, and run Soul Storms for Uniques and rare drops. Salvage spare gear for emeralds and spend them before the 9,999 cap.

The fastest farming in Minecraft Dungeons 2 is boss rematches for XP, dungeons and rifts for echo shards, and Soul Storms for Uniques. Some of the numbers below come from players in launch week, so treat those as reported. The list of every reported figure sits at the end.

Checked in-game on October 1, 2026.

How do you farm XP fast?

Re-fight bosses. Every boss can be fought again after you beat it: return to its area and use its Boss Totem. Boss kills are the best XP source for both your hero and your talismans. Boss XP scales with how far into the campaign the boss sits, so the latest boss you can reliably beat is the best one to repeat.

Replaying a boss at its Boss Totem pays additional rewards, so the same run also feeds your other goals. The boss guide has attack patterns, and Howling Woods has the Witch boss you can rematch at will.

Where do you get echo shards?

Clear dungeons and rifts. Tough blue soul-empowered mobs also drop shards, and the empowered mob guide covers how to handle them. Dungeons and rifts are the steady source: finishing one gives you an obsidian chest with gear, echo shards and enchantment books.

Rifts spawn at random around the map, so keep an eye out as you travel. Overworld bosses and Soul Storms pay shards too. Echo shards cap at 100, so spend them before you farm more.

Shards level your merchants and pay for enchanting, so they go fast. The Enchantsmith guide shows what each merchant level unlocks.

How do Soul Storms work?

Storms roll through the regions on a 40-minute cycle, and each one runs for 20 minutes until you break its 3 generators. Each generator and the storm itself drop chests with three-effect items. The full timer, region order and clear route are in the Soul Storm guide.

How do you farm emeralds?

Salvage every item you will not use. Salvaging turns spare gear into emeralds with no other cost, and bosses and dungeons drop emeralds as well. Do not hoard: the emerald cap is 9,999 and anything past it is lost.

Emeralds buy merchant stock, and echo shards level the merchants so better stock appears. If you are near the cap, spend on gear or enchanting first, the Enchantsmith guide has the costs we have so far.

Where do Uniques drop?

Uniques come from random loot and side-quest rewards. Merchants never sell them.

Players put rough drop chances at about 20% for a Soul Storm reward chest, about 17% for bosses, about 10% for fancy chests and mini-bosses, about 5% for regular mobs and about 4% for quest rewards. That makes storms and boss rematches the best places to look.

Each region has its own Unique pool, so farm the region whose gear you want. The regions hub links every region page, and the map’s “View Area Items” list shows what is left in an area. Rerolling a Unique’s reroll-able effect needs a level 3 Blacksmith, and rerolls are permanent. Its signature effect cannot be changed.

Which gear boosts your loot?

Four effects raise drops: Raider, Looter, Luck and Prospector, plus the Lucky Clover and Looter’s Charm talismans. Stack them when the content is safe, and swap back to damage gear for hard storms. The loot gear guide has the numbers and a slot-by-slot setup.

What are the Sift states?

The Sift runs in three states: Flow, Endure and Thrive. For farming, the useful ones are Thrive, which refills souls and artifact timers faster, and Endure, which hardens enemies and shuts off soul regen. Flow is the baseline. The Sift guide covers them in full. Pick a state that suits your build, for example Thrive if your artifacts need souls, and skip Endure on an underpowered character.

Quick FAQ

What is the best way to level up quickly? Re-fight the latest boss you can beat at its Boss Totem. Boss XP scales with campaign progress.

Can you buy Uniques from merchants? No. Merchants never sell Uniques, so you have to find them in the world.

Do you need to finish the campaign first? Not for dungeons, rifts or emeralds. Soul Storms and boss rematches are easier once you are further in, since you have better gear.

Does farming work in co-op? Merchant levels and stock are per player, so each friend levels their own. See the co-op guide for how parties work.
