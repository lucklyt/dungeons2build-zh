  • Minecraft Dungeons 2 Beginner's Guide: How to Play (Start Here)

    Start with an online hero, finish the 鱿鱼海岸 tutorial, reach Brave Haven, then unlock the merchant, Enchantsmith and Blacksmith in that order. Fight at range, jump for safety, keep every gear slot filled and pick Normal unless you want a gentler start.

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 has 31 armor sets, 13 of them unique. Twisted Warden leads for melee damage, Woodsprite for tanking and companions, Hivemind and Fly Agaric for support, Mad Sifter and Oracle for artifact builds.

  • Best Loot Gear in Minecraft Dungeons 2: Raider, Looter, Luck, Prospector

    Four effects boost loot: Raider, Looter, Luck and Prospector. Add the Lucky Clover and Looter's Charm talismans, and run loot gear only when your damage build is already safe.

  • How Power Level and Item Power Work, and How to Level Up Fast

    Hero level comes from XP, item power comes from your gear. Empty slots drag item power down, so fill every slot, run the early 蜂巢田野 quests, and use the merchant refresh loop to turn emeralds into power.

  • All 9 Secret Talismans and How to Unlock the Mosstrosity Boss

    Eight secret talismans sit at fixed spots across the map, three of them tied to random dungeon or rift rooms. Find all eight and a secret door opens in 呼啸森林, leading to the Mosstrosity boss and the ninth talisman, Wonderful Wheat.

  • Soul Storms in Minecraft Dungeons 2: Timer, Regions and Rewards

    A storm starts every 40 minutes, lasts 20 and rotates through seven regions in a fixed order. Break the 3 generators, loot each generator chest, then open the storm chest.

    Minecraft Dungeons 2 has 24 talismans, and we list effects for 23 of them. They level to 3 through use. Armadillo Amulet, Healing Heart and Sigil of Beeswax lead for survival, Fist of Iron for melee damage.

  • How to Get Unique Items in Minecraft Dungeons 2

    Unique gear in Minecraft Dungeons 2 is guaranteed by some side quests, can drop when you replay bosses at their Boss Totem, and also enters the general loot pool, more so near endgame and in storm areas. Merchants never sell it.

  • How to Farm XP, Echo Shards and Uniques in Minecraft Dungeons 2

    Re-fight bosses for XP, clear dungeons and rifts for echo shards, and run Soul Storms for Uniques and rare drops. Salvage spare gear for emeralds and spend them before the 9,999 cap.

    The Minecraft Dungeons 2 campaign runs 鱿鱼海岸 and Brave Haven, 蜂巢田野, 呼啸森林, the Deep Dark, 呼啸森林 again, 多雨平原, 冰封高地, three Sift regions, the Illager Stronghold and the final boss. Postgame adds side quests and Soul Storm.
