Grade colour key

Every card wears one grade: a colour and a label. Weapons, armor and talismans use rarity. Artifacts have no rarity, so they use a category. Enchantments use a grade plus levels I to III.

Rarity (weapons, armor, talismans)

  • Unique Named gear with a fixed effect.
  • Common Base weapons, armor and talismans. Drops can roll a higher rarity.

Artifact category

  • Damage Artifacts that hurt or boost your hits.
  • Crowd control Artifacts that freeze, root, stun or knock down.
  • Support Artifacts that heal or buff allies.
  • Defense Artifacts that block or toughen you.
  • Mobility Artifacts that speed you up or hide you.
  • Utility Effect not listed yet.

Quick 常见问题

Is this every item in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

Not yet. It covers the gear catalogued in the first days after the September 29, 2026 launch, and many entries still lack numbers. We add stats as they are documented.

Why do some entries describe a system instead of naming an item?

Two entries describe how a system works (the four-piece armor split and the artifact slots) rather than a single item, so you can see the rules next to the gear.

How are rarity and grade shown, and are there drop rates?

Rarity is listed on every weapon, armor piece and talisman. Launch data marks Unique gear, and everything else is a base item shown as Common. Artifacts have no rarity, so they carry a category instead (damage, crowd control, support and so on), and every enchantment shows as Standard with levels I to III. Drop rates are not documented yet, so we do not list them.
