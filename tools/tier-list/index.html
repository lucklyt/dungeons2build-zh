Minecraft Dungeons 2 Tier List

The best gear at launch is Tornado Blade and Pride of the Plains for weapons, Death Cap Mushroom, Fighter's Flute and Firework Arrow for artifacts, Armadillo Amulet for talismans, and Frost Crescent and Healing Smite for enchantments. Every pick has a reason, and anything without enough evidence sits in Unranked yet.

Launch-week info, last checked Oct 1, 2026. We update this page as patches and new data land.

How we rank

What counts as evidence

Three things: documented numbers (effect percentages, Soul cost, cooldown), what players say about the item in launch-week footage, and what press guides call a favorite. A tier needs at least two of them, or one very clear number.

How an item gets a tier

S means the item does a lot for a low cost and players keep reaching for it. A is strong but tied to a playstyle. B is solid in the right build. C is niche. D means the numbers or the game's own systems make it a weak slot. Ranks are within a category, so a B artifact is not compared to an S weapon.

What goes in Unranked yet

Anything with no listed effect, no numbers or only a press mention. We would rather leave it out than guess. Items that are reported but not confirmed also sit there, labelled.

What this list cannot see

Launch data does not publish weapon damage yet, drop rates are unknown and most player footage comes from early-access builds. Treat this as a launch week snapshot. We will move items as real damage numbers and patches arrive.

Tier cutoffs in plain words

  • SDoes a lot for a low cost and players keep reaching for it.
  • AStrong, but tied to a playstyle.
  • BSolid in the right build.
  • CNiche.

Tier list questions

What is the best weapon in Minecraft Dungeons 2 right now?

Tornado Blade and Pride of the Plains rank S in launch week. Tornado Blade adds a swirl worth 60% of weapon damage on every swing, and Pride of the Plains does 40% more damage to your main target. Launch data does not publish base weapon damage yet, so this ranks the listed unique effects, not raw damage.

How was this tier list made?

Each pick needs at least two kinds of evidence: documented numbers (effect percentages, Soul cost, cooldown), what players say in launch week footage, and what press guides call a favorite. Anything with less goes in Unranked yet instead of getting a guessed tier.

Why are so many items unranked?

88 items are unranked because they have no listed effect, no numbers, or are reported but not confirmed. Base weapon types are the largest group, since nobody has published their damage. They move into a tier once there is data.

Will the tiers change?

Yes. This is Launch week v1, dated October 1, 2026. Balance patches and published damage numbers will move items, and the date on this page changes each time we update it.

What are the best artifacts and talismans in Minecraft Dungeons 2?

Death Cap Mushroom, Fighter's Flute and Firework Arrow lead the artifact tier, and Armadillo Amulet leads talismans. Firework Arrow works best next to the Humbling Horn, and the Armadillo Amulet helps every build because it cuts roll cooldown by 20%.
